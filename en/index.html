Scope of Duties & Services

1

Legal Guardianship (Rechtliche Betreuung)

Empowering Self-Determination Through Comprehensive Support

Managing complex medical, financial, and administrative challenges can often feel overwhelming and obscure the path forward. My goal is to handle the legal, administrative, and logistical burdens—so you have the clarity, dignity, and stability needed to focus on your personal growth. By safeguarding your self-determination and building a secure foundation, we create the space for you to discover where you belong and build a life where you can truly flourish.

Healthcare & Care Organization

Obtaining medical consent, liaising with physicians, clinics, and care teams, and organizing outpatient care or appropriate housing arrangements while strictly safeguarding individual self-determination.

Financial Management & Administrative Representation

Managing financial affairs, overseeing bank accounts, securing social benefit entitlements (e.g., SGB II, V, XI, XII), and resolving pension and insurance matters.

Housing & Official Representation

Securing and maintaining tenancy, representing clients before landlords, public authorities, and government agencies, and managing necessary home adaptations or relocations.

Coaching & Decision Support

Career Coaching

Navigating critical career transitions and academic milestones requires more than just meeting requirements—it demands clarity, strategic alignment, and self-discovery. Whether you are transitioning between academia and industry, untangling complex decision-making processes, or adapting to a new educational system, true progress happens when you align your unique strengths with the right environment. My coaching is designed to help you cut through administrative and strategic complexity, providing structured guidance so you can make confident decisions, thrive academically, and build a fulfilling career in Germany and Europe.

Strategic Positioning

Strategic positioning for transitioning from academia to industry (or vice versa), profile sharpening, and application strategies—including fully funded coaching (via AVGS vouchers from the Employment Agency/Jobcenter in collaboration with certified partners).

Academic Coaching & Mentoring

Structuring research projects, assisting with proposals/exposés, guiding the search for doctoral supervisors (Doctoral Supervisor Placement), and preparing for defenses (Disputationen).

International Student Guidance & European Higher Education

Specialized support for international students and scholars navigating admission, academic transfer, and continuation of studies in Germany and the wider EU. Services include credential assessment alignment, university application strategy, visa/administrative orientation, and social-cultural integration into the European academic landscape.

Decision-Making Support

Systematic evaluation of options during goal conflicts, objective resolution of deadlock situations, and structured decision-making at critical career and life crossroads.

Core Philosophy

When Joker was released, viewers instinctively projected themselves onto Arthur Fleck, viewing his tragic spiral through the lens of their own quiet struggles. But that reaction misses a more uncomfortable truth: most of us aren't the tragic anti-hero pushed to the edge. We are the indifferent citizens of Gotham—the onlookers and silent systems whose collective neglect creates the conditions for the breakdown in the first place.

Society frequently uses labels like "crazy" to dismiss people who think outside established boxes. History is filled with visionaries who were initially treated as unstable or unfit:

Plagued by severe depression, anxiety, and psychotic episodes, Van Gogh was viewed by his community as a dangerous madman. Local residents in Arles even signed a petition to have him forcibly removed from his home.

Vincent van Gogh

(1853–1890)

While celebrated earlier in life, Tesla’s severe Obsessive-Compulsive Disorder (OCD), hypersensitivities, and increasingly ambitious visions (like wireless power transmission) led the public and investors to dismiss him in his later years as a "mad scientist." Ostracized by major financial backers, he spent his final years living in relative isolation and poverty in New York hotel rooms.

Nikola Tesla

(1856–1943)

A brilliant French sculptor whose talent rivaled (and often contributed to) Auguste Rodin. Her intense demeanor, refusal to conform to 19th-century gender expectations, and later paranoia led her family to view her as completely insane. She was involuntarily committed to a psychiatric asylum by her family in 1913, where she remained isolated for 30 years until her death, despite doctors suggesting she could be released.

Camille Claudel

(1864–1943)

True alignment isn't about changing yourself to fit a mold.

While medical and psychological conditions can certainly impair our ability to fit in, true alignment isn't about changing yourself to fit a mold. It is about self-discovery—finding the environments where you can thrive, and channeling your efforts into that purpose. This is the core of my practice, and a promise I am committed to keeping.
